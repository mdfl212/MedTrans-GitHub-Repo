## 💡 Worried about trying MedTrans? Start with these FAQs

You **do not need to be technical** to try MedTrans. These are common questions students ask before getting started.

<details>
  <summary><strong>Can I use MedTrans on my laptop and then continue on my iPad or phone?</strong></summary>

  **Yes, in many cases.** If you sign in to the same AI account, some settings and installed skills may be available across devices—but this depends on the AI platform and how the skill was added. If MedTrans does not appear on your other device, download the skill there or upload the required MedTrans files again. Make sure the lecture PDF is accessible on that device, too.
</details>

<details>
  <summary><strong>Do I need to install it separately on every device?</strong></summary>

  Not always. If your AI platform syncs the skill with your account, you may only need to add it once. If you used a one-time file upload in a chat, it may only be available in that chat—so you may need to attach the files again on another device or in a new chat.
</details>

<details>
  <summary><strong>Will trying MedTrans change or delete my original lecture slides?</strong></summary>

  MedTrans is intended to help create a separate Word reviewer. Keep your original lecture PDF and save a copy of the generated DOCX. Always check the reviewer against the original before relying on it.
</details>

<details>
  <summary><strong>Do I need to know how to code?</strong></summary>

  **No.** Follow the beginner tutorial, choose the AI assistant you already use, and copy the short prompt. You do not need to edit code.
</details>

<details>
  <summary><strong>Will it work exactly the same in every AI assistant?</strong></summary>

  Not exactly. Menus, available skill features, usage limits, and document formatting vary by platform and account. The tutorial shows common ways to get started, but your screen and final document may look a little different.
</details>

<details>
  <summary><strong>Do I have to pay?</strong></summary>

  The MedTrans files are available from this page, but your AI assistant may have its own plan requirements, usage limits, or paid features. Check the options available in your account before starting a long reviewer.
</details>

**Still unsure?** Open the [beginner tutorial]({{ '/tutorial/' | relative_url }}) and follow it one step at a time. You can start with one lecture and see how it goes.

## Start here

1. **Prepare your lecture PDF.** Run OCR only if you cannot select or copy the text.
2. **Load MedTrans** in your chosen AI assistant using its Skills or upload option.
3. **Copy the simple prompt** from the tutorial, attach your lecture, and answer the question about figures.
4. **Check the finished Word reviewer** against the original slides before sharing it.
