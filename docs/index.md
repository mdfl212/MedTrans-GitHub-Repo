## Start here

1. **Prepare your lecture PDF.** Run OCR only if you cannot select or copy the text.
2. **Load MedTrans** in your chosen AI assistant using its Skills or upload option.
3. **Copy the simple prompt** from the tutorial, attach your lecture, and answer the question about figures.
4. **Check the finished Word reviewer** against the original slides before sharing it.

## Made for MD201–MD207

MedTrans includes matching templates and colors for MD201, MD202, MD203, MD204, MD205, MD206, and MD207. Choose the course code that matches your class.
